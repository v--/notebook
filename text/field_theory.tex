\chapter{Field theory}\label{ch:field_theory}

Compared to more general \hyperref[def:ring]{rings} and \hyperref[def:module]{modules}, \hyperref[def:field]{fields} and \hyperref[def:vector_space]{vector spaces} are particularly well-behaved. For example, \cref{thm:finite_fields} shows how a unique field with \( p^n \) exists for every prime power, while \fullref{thm:vector_space_basis_existence} allows studying a vector space via its bases.

This enables some special topics like \fullref{sec:field_extensions} and \fullref{sec:primitive_roots}, which in turn leads to further applications like \fullref{sec:error_correcting_codes}.
